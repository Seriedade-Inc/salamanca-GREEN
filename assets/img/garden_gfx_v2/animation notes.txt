If this distribution contains animations, they are structured as "seq" followed by a letter, and then the sprite name.
This means seqA_bush000 is followed by seqB_bush000 and then seqC_bush000 (assuming it exists).

Animations may take several forms - 
* seqA,B,C,D - a ping-pong sequence, often used for wind. The correct animation is A,B,C,D,C,B, (repeats)
Some may prefer to hold the first and last frames (A & D) for added effect (repeats)
I suggest 0.2s per frame

* seqA,B,C - used for liquid animations. The correct animation is A,B,C,B, (repeats)
If you can vary the timing, I suggest 0.6s, 0.4s, 0.6s, 0.4s, (repeats)
